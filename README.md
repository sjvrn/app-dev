# My Favorite Series: Banana Fish

**Banana Fish** is a crime thriller *anime* based on the *manga* by Akimi Yoshida. It follows a street gang leader in New York City who uncovers a dangerous conspiracy.

---

## Quick Facts

| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Original Creator | Akimi Yoshida |
| Anime Studio | MAPPA |
| Episodes | 24 |
| Genre | Crime, Drama, Action, Thriller |
| Setting | New York City |

## Main Characters

1. **Ash Lynx** - the young and brilliant leader of a street gang
2. **Eiji Okumura** - a kind Japanese photographer's assistant who gets pulled into Ash's world
3. **Shorter Wong** - Ash's loyal friend
4. **Max Glenreed** - a journalist who helps investigate the case

## Why I Love It

- Strong, well-written characters
- A gripping mystery that keeps you guessing
- A touching friendship at the center of the story
- Emotional and memorable storytelling

> Banana Fish is a story about friendship, trauma, and hope.

## Fun Detail

In the story, `Banana Fish` is the name of a mysterious drug that drives the whole plot.
